Let's clear things up

A Costume Doesn't Make Someone Mean.

Stories and scary movies sometimes show clowns as villains, but that's only one kind of fictional character. Real people are more complicated than a costume or a makeup design.

  • Judge people by their actions, not just their appearance.
  • It's okay to feel nervous around something unfamiliar.
  • Kindness, respect, and boundaries matter for everyone.
Our HappyClown promise

Make Room for More Kindness!

Whether you laugh at a clown's joke, cheer for a performer, or simply treat someone with respect, you can help make the world friendlier. Remember: clowns aren't all mean, and nobody should be judged by looks alone.
